Historic Missions

Apollo 11 Moon Landing

NASA

Date: July 20-21, 1969

Astronauts: Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin, Michael Collins

Achievement: First humans to walk on the Moon

Apollo 11 was the defining moment in space exploration. Neil Armstrong became the first human to set foot on the Moon, followed by Buzz Aldrin. Michael Collins orbited the Moon while they explored the lunar surface.

👨‍🚀
"That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind" - Neil Armstrong

Mars Rovers - Curiosity & Perseverance

NASA

Active Since: Curiosity (2012), Perseverance (2021)

Objective: Explore Mars surface and search for signs of past life

Currently exploring Gale Crater and Jezero Crater respectively.

International Space Station

International Partnership

Operational Since: 1998

Objective: Continuous human presence in low Earth orbit

A collaborative effort involving NASA, ESA, Roscosmos, and other space agencies.

Artemis Program

NASA

Objective: Return humans to the Moon

Goal: Establish sustainable lunar exploration and prepare for Mars

Artemis I successfully launched in November 2022 in an uncrewed test flight.

About Space Mission Explorer

Space Mission Explorer is an interactive educational platform dedicated to space exploration and discovery. It combines visualizations of our solar system with comprehensive information about historic space missions, from the Apollo 11 Moon landing to modern Mars rovers.

Our mission is to inspire the next generation of space explorers and scientists by providing engaging, interactive content about humanity's achievements in space.
